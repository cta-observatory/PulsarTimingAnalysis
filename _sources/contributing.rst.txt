.. _contributing:

************
Contributing
************

Bug reports, feature requests, questions and contributions are welcome through
the `issue tracker <https://github.com/cta-observatory/PulsarTimingAnalysis/issues>`__
and pull requests.

The contribution guidelines (development environment, tests, code style and
review process) are described in
`CONTRIBUTING.md <https://github.com/cta-observatory/PulsarTimingAnalysis/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md>`__,
and all the participants are expected to follow the
`code of conduct <https://github.com/cta-observatory/PulsarTimingAnalysis/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md>`__.
