# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the science.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Publishing private information of others without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainers of the project (see the contact information in
`pyproject.toml`). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly, respecting the privacy of the reporter.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all the project spaces (issue tracker,
pull requests, discussions) and when an individual is officially representing
the project.
